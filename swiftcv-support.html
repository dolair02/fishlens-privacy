Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Resume Builder work?

Tap "AI Resume Builder" on the Home screen, answer a few questions about your background and target role, and the AI will draft a complete resume for you. You can then edit and refine it in the editor.

Can I switch templates without losing my content?

Yes - switching between templates (Executive, Modern Minimal, Classic Pro, and others) keeps all your entered content; only the visual style changes.

How do I export my resume?

Open your resume in the editor and tap "PDF" at the top to export a formatted PDF ready to send or upload to job applications.

How do I track a job application?

Go to the Applications tab and tap "Add" to log a role. Move it between Saved, Applied, Interview, and other stages as your process progresses.

How do I delete my account or data?

Go to Account settings and select Delete Account to permanently remove your profile and data.
