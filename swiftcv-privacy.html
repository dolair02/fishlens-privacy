Privacy Policy - SwiftCV

Last updated: August 12, 2026

SwiftCV ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (or sign-in credentials) when you create an account.
  • Resume content: work history, education, skills, contact details, and any other information you enter into your resumes.
  • Job application data: job titles, companies, application stage, and notes you add to track your applications.
  • Usage and device data: app interactions, crash logs, and device information, used to improve reliability and performance.
  • Payment information: if you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive or store your card details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To generate, edit, and format your resumes, including AI-assisted drafting.
  • To track your job applications and provide reminders and status updates.
  • To communicate with you about your account.
  • To maintain and improve the app's performance and security.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, AI resume generation, analytics), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only when required by law.

Links to external job boards (such as Indeed, CareerBuilder, or Monster) open those sites directly; we do not share your resume data with them unless you choose to do so yourself.

Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete your resumes and application entries at any time within the app.
  • You can delete your account from Account settings; this permanently removes your profile and personal data, except where we're required to retain it for legal reasons.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app's features. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

Children's Privacy

SwiftCV is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us
If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at support@swiftcvapp.com.
