Frequently Asked Questions

Why didn't the app identify my fish correctly?

Identification accuracy depends on photo clarity, lighting, and angle. For best results, take a clear, well-lit photo showing the whole fish. If the AI is uncertain or you'd like a second opinion, use the "Request Expert Verification" option on the identification result.

How do I upgrade to FishLens Unlimited?

Go to Account → Plan and tap "Upgrade to Unlimited." Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and can be managed or canceled anytime in your device's Subscription settings.

Can I use FishLens without an internet connection?

Yes — maps, tides, and trip data you've previously loaded are cached for offline use. New fish identifications require an internet connection.

How do I export or delete my data?

Go to Account → "Export my data" to download your catches, trips, and gear. To permanently delete your account and data, go to Account → Delete Account.

How do I report an issue with the app?

Email us using the contact below with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version if possible — that helps us resolve it faster.
